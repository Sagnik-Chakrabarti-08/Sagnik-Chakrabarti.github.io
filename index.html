Research

SRM Institute of Science and Technology · Supervisor: Dr. Jesline Daniel

Cataract detection with a smartphone

Cataracts are a leading cause of preventable blindness, and screening usually needs a trained ophthalmologist and slit-lamp or fundus equipment. I benchmarked raw, histogram-equalized and CLAHE preprocessing across ResNet18, GoogleNet, ResNet101 and DenseNet201 in PyTorch, then added Grad-CAM heatmaps and a Streamlit app that returns a diagnosis and explanation in under 20 seconds on consumer hardware.

98.33%test accuracy
98%precision and sensitivity
100%specificity

Dept. of Biomedical Engineering, SRMIST · Supervisor: Dr. Jaligam Murali Mohan

Spinal abnormality detection from MRI

Disc herniation is increasingly common in young adults, and MRI reads are slow and depend on scarce radiologists. I built an OpenCV pipeline and used the Spinal Cord Toolbox to segment the cord on T2-weighted lumbar MRI. Shape features such as ellipticity and flatness feed a binary classifier for normal versus herniated discs, with interpretability and robustness as priorities.

The result is "Spine Detect Pro", a Flask web app with real-time upload, instant classification and clinical safety disclaimers, aimed at rural centers without radiologists.

Projects

SRMIST · IEEE paper

Multilingual sentiment dashboard

A context-retaining crawler and rule-based transliteration normalize Romanized Bengali, Persian and Arabic to native scripts. XLM-RoBERTa classifies sentiment, zero-shot BART-large-MNLI identifies intent, and Sentence-BERT filters off-topic replies using language-dependent cosine-similarity cutoffs. Results appear in an interactive Streamlit dashboard.
